Official APIs/permission hooks establish feasibility, not proof of Travel OS isolation or add-on compatibility. If false, hosting, identity, backend boundaries and maintenance change. Validate pinned framework/ERPNext/localization/MCP/WhatsApp versions, custom-app hosting, denied direct requests/exports/files, combined roles and reliable connector recovery before adoption. [Evaluation](erpnext-frappe-evaluation.md), [0011](../decisions/0011-configurable-users-roles-and-permissions.md). Separate B2C/B2B entries still do not resolve AS-002.

FB-056 extends AS-027 validation to the [standard-app combination](frappe-standard-apps-evaluation.md): pin compatible CRM/ERPNext/optional app versions, choose one lead/deal and catalog owner, compare same-site versus remote synchronization, and reconcile CRM hierarchy, Helpdesk/Drive sharing and Insights source permissions with custom travel scopes. Published handoffs establish candidate reuse, not a tested combined deployment; failures can change the app shortlist and headless development effort.

Before using a material assumption, add or update an entry with its implication and validation path. Suggested statuses: provisional, awaiting evidence, confirmed, rejected, superseded. Confirmation requires evidence or a clear user/client answer; retain the source and update affected docs. Link detailed decisions rather than duplicating their rationale.
