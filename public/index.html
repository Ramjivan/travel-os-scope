Pillar 01 • Sales & Telephony

Omnichannel CRM & Communications Suite

⚡ 40% Faster First Response • 100% Chat Retention

01. Ad Auto-Capture

Meta & Google Ads Webhooks

Instant lead injection into visual Kanban pipeline with duplicate phone/email detection and round-robin allocation.

02. Web WhatsApp

Official Two-Way Team Inbox

Official WhatsApp Business API integration inside the browser. Dispatch proposals, payment links, and vouchers in 1 click.

Exotel IVR & Click-to-Call

Click-to-call directly from customer records. Inbound smart IVR recognizes returning travelers and routes to assigned rep.

04. AI Intelligence

Whisper Speech-to-Text & Summaries

Every phone call is automatically recorded, transcribed to text, and summarized into 3-5 bullet points against the lead file.

Pillar 02 • Finance & Revenue Protection

Travel Accounting, Invoicing & Real-Time P&L

🎯 100% Margin Transparency • Zero Leakage

Eliminates spreadsheet-based blind pricing. Connects client collections directly to supplier purchase costs, enforcing margin floors, automated dual-model GST tax invoices, and real-time ledger settlement.

01. Margin Engine

Per-Booking Real-Time P&L

Instant gross margin % and net profit calculation (Client Sell Price - Total Supplier Cost) visible to managers prior to locking.

02. Indian Tax Engine

Dual-Model GST Invoicing

Automated generation of 5% Tour Package invoices (without ITC) and 18% Agent Service invoices with compliant SAC codes and GSTIN.

03. Digital Collections

Razorpay / UPI Payment Links

Generate WhatsApp-shareable payment links with real-time webhook callbacks that immediately update outstanding balance dues.

04. Ledger Settlement

Supplier Payables & Tally Export

Track vendor payables, manual UTR numbers, advance receipts, and export complete audit ledgers directly into Tally Prime or CSV.

📊
Pillar 03 • Intelligence & Oversight

Real-Time Reporting & MIS Analytics

📈 3-Click Executive Oversight • Live Dashboards

Puts live operational metrics at management's fingertips. No more waiting until the end of the month for reports — know your top sales reps, highest-margin destinations, and pipeline conversion bottlenecks instantly.

01. Rep Performance

Executive KPI Cockpit

Personalized rep views tracking active leads, follow-up compliance rate, conversion %, and first response times.

02. Revenue Health

Company Management MIS

Aggregate gross sales, realized net profit, destination revenue breakdown, and marketing ad-spend ROI analytics.

03. Pipeline Funnel

Conversion Funnel & Lost Reasons

Pinpoint exact stages where leads drop off. Categorize lost deals (pricing, competitor, timing) to adjust future markups.

04. Operations Dispatch

Movement & Seat Fill-Rates

Daily operational calendar of passenger arrivals, hotel check-ins, and pre-purchased series air seat depletion rates.

🛡️
Pillar 04 • Security & Compliance

Tamper-Proof Audit Trail & Enterprise Governance

🔒 100% Immutable Event Logs • DPDP 2023 Ready

Provides foolproof security and compliance. Prevents unauthorized rate tampering, logs every price revision, protects client passport data under the India DPDP Act 2023, and insulates against card data liabilities.

01. Immutable Logs

Tamper-Proof Audit Trail

Every action is cryptographically logged: User ID, Action Name, Record Modified, Exact Timestamp, and IP Address.

02. Manager Oversight

Supervisory Inspection Layer

Supervisors can listen to any call recording, inspect WhatsApp chats, view quote revision diffs, and approve discount overrides.

03. Strict Permissions

Role-Based Access (RBAC)

Granular role separation across Super Admin, Sales Manager, Sales Rep, Operations Executive, and Accounts Team.

04. Privacy Compliance

India DPDP Act 2023 & PCI DSS

Passport masking (******4589), right-to-forget anonymization, S3 KMS encryption, and hosted PCI DSS SAQ-A tokenization.
